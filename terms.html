Before you rely on a result in flight

Whizwheel works the E6B problems (wind, airspeed, altitude, time, speed and distance, adding and subtracting flight times, fuel, weight and balance, climb and descent, unit conversion) from the figures you enter. Its answers can only be as good as those figures, and it cannot see your aircraft or the weather.

  • Check every figure you intend to fly with against the aircraft's approved flight manual or POH, its weight-and-balance records and its performance charts. Where Whizwheel and that approved data differ, follow the approved data.
