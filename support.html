Support

Whizwheel for iPhone and iPad · last revised 2026-10-19

Whizwheel opens on a board of twelve tools. Tap one, type the figures you already have, and the answer updates with every keystroke. Nothing needs a connection, so it behaves the same on the ramp and in airplane mode.

The board

WINDWind correction angle, heading and ground speed; or the wind itself from what you flew. Runway components. Add the magnetic variation and it also gives the magnetic course and heading.
TASCalibrated, true and equivalent airspeed and Mach.
DAPressure and density altitude, plus the cold-temperature correction and true altitude.
TSDTime, speed or distance from the other two.
TIMEAdding and subtracting flight times in hours, minutes and seconds.
FUELFuel required for a given time, endurance, and what the fuel weighs.
W&BMoment, centre of gravity and limits, with aircraft profiles you can save.
DESWhere to start down and the descent rate, from a gradient in ft/nm, % or degrees; the climb rate a SID or missed-approach gradient asks for.
HOLDWhich holding entry to fly: direct, teardrop or parallel.
PNRPoint of no return and equal time point, with different ground speeds out and back.
1:60From distance flown, distance off course and distance remaining: track error, closing angle and the turn to fly direct, solved with trigonometry rather than the 1-in-60 shortcut.
CONVDistance, speed, weight, volume, pressure and temperature.

Moving a figure to the next tool

Some answers are the input of the next problem. When one is available, the next tool shows a button naming the value and the tool it came from. Tap it to fill the field; until you do, nothing is changed for you. The four hand-offs are:

  • pressure altitude (and temperature, if entered) from DA to TAS
  • true airspeed from TAS to WIND
  • ground speed from WIND to TSD
  • leg time from TSD to FUEL

Each hand-off holds only its most recent value (for DA to TAS, the most recent pair), and all of them are gone when the App is closed.

Typing a time

The time fields in TSD, FUEL and PNR read a plain number as minutes: 45 is 45 minutes and 8.5 is 8 minutes 30 seconds. A colon switches to hours: 8:56 is 8 hours 56 minutes, so 8 minutes 56 seconds is 0:08:56. Under the field the App spells out what it understood, so a slip shows straight away.

The TIME tool, which adds or subtracts two flight times, reads a plain number differently: there it is hours (1.5 is 1:30:00), and minutes take a colon (0:45). Anything written with a colon means the same on every screen.

When a figure disagrees with a rule of thumb

Cockpit rules of thumb are approximations. Whizwheel solves the underlying equations instead: density altitude from the standard atmosphere rather than 120 ft per degree above ISA, true airspeed with compressibility rather than 2% per thousand feet. At 5,000 ft and 30 °C that is about 190 ft of density altitude; at 250 kt calibrated, FL250 and −35 °C, about 12 kt of true airspeed. The cold-temperature correction uses the ICAO Doc 8168 equation rather than 4% per 10 °C, and the 1:60 tool uses trigonometry rather than the 1-in-60 shortcut. For these four, a gap against the rule of thumb is expected, and it grows with altitude, temperature or angle; the figures above are examples, not limits.

To check one of those four, compare it with a table or a full calculation for the same conditions, not with the rule of thumb. If it still differs, or another tool disagrees with your own working by more than rounding, it may be a fault: please write to us with the tool, every figure on the screen, and where your own figure came from (a POH table, a mechanical E6B, a test question).

Aircraft profiles

Weight-and-balance profiles live in Whizwheel's storage on this device. Whizwheel never syncs or sends them anywhere, and removing the App removes them. The privacy notice lists what a profile holds and how an iOS device backup may include it.

Knowledge tests

Phones and tablets stay outside the FAA testing room, so Whizwheel cannot go in with you. Take an approved flight computer, or use the one the test centre provides.

What it leaves out

No maps, weather, navigation log or flight plan filing. Keeping to calculation is why it needs no network, asks for no permissions and is a single purchase.
