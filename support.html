Updated: 2026-08-25

How to use

  1. Open the App. The first screen lists all eight tools; nothing is hidden behind a menu.
  2. Pick a tool and type the values you have. Results appear as you type.
  3. For weight and balance, add your stations, enter the limits, then save the aircraft profile so it is one tap away next time.

FAQ

Why do the numbers differ slightly from my mechanical E6B or a rule of thumb?
Whizwheel solves the equations rather than the approximations. Density altitude comes from the standard atmosphere rather than "120 ft per degree above ISA", and true airspeed accounts for compressibility rather than "2% per thousand feet". At 5,000 ft and 30 °C the density altitude differs by roughly 190 ft; at 250 kt calibrated, FL250 and −35 °C the true airspeed differs by about 12 kt.

Can I use it with no signal?
Yes, always. The App contains no network code, so coverage makes no difference.

Where are my aircraft profiles kept?
On this device only. They are not synced, backed up to us, or transmitted anywhere. Deleting the App deletes them.

Can I use it in an FAA knowledge test?
No. Personal electronic devices are not allowed in the testing room. Use an approved mechanical E6B or the one supplied at the test centre.

Why is there no map, weather or flight plan?
Whizwheel is a calculator. Keeping it to calculation is what lets it work offline, ask for no permissions, and stay a single purchase.
