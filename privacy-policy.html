Privacy notice

Whizwheel for iPhone and iPad · last revised 2026-10-03

Whizwheel is an E6B flight computer that runs entirely on your iPhone or iPad. This page lists what it keeps, what it does not use, and how information about you could reach its developer, Naruberio LLC ("we", "us"). The App itself sends us nothing. The only thing that comes to us directly from you is an email you decide to send.

At a glance

Internet connectionNot used. Every tool works in airplane mode.
PermissionsNone requested: no location, camera, photos, contacts or microphone.
Account or sign-inThere isn't one.
Ads, analytics, crash reporting, trackingNone built in.
Saved on your deviceAircraft profiles you choose to save.
What reaches usNothing from the App itself. From you directly, only emails you write to us. Apple separately sends us App Store totals (see Buying Whizwheel).

Aircraft profiles

A profile keeps these items from the weight-and-balance screen: the profile name, each station's label, weight and arm, which stations are fuel tanks, and your maximum weight with the forward and aft CG limits, always in pounds and inches. It goes into Whizwheel's own storage area on the device. Delete a profile inside the App and the App no longer keeps or shows it; delete the App and every profile goes with it.

If iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer is switched on, iOS may copy this storage area into the device backup, as it does for most apps. That copy sits with Apple or on your computer. We have no way to open it.

Numbers you type into the tools

Apart from what you save as an aircraft profile, courses, airspeeds, altitudes, times and fuel figures stay in memory only while the App is open. They are not written to storage, and they are never sent anywhere.

Buying Whizwheel

Whizwheel has one price on the App Store. There are no in-app purchases, no subscription and no payment code inside the App. Apple takes the payment. What Apple passes to us are App Store totals, such as sales by country, never buyer details, so your name, Apple Account and card never come to us. If you have turned on Share With App Developers in your device settings, Apple also gives us crash reports and usage statistics that do not identify you. Apple's privacy policy explains how Apple treats payment information.

Emailing us

Write to konpei.work@gmail.com and we receive your address and whatever the message contains. We use them to reply to you and for nothing else. We do not sell or share them, and we will delete the conversation if you ask.

Children

Whizwheel gathers no information from anyone, so it gathers none from children. It shows no advertising and no outside content.

Requests under GDPR, CCPA or Japan's APPI

The App passes us no personal data, so for the App there is nothing for us to export, correct or erase. The profiles on your device are yours to edit or remove at any time. For emails you have sent us, write to the same address and we will carry out your request.

Revisions

If the way Whizwheel handles information changes, this notice is revised and the date at the top moves with it.
