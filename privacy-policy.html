Privacy Policy — Whizwheel

Effective date: 2026-08-25

Whizwheel (the "App") is published by Naruberio LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the App does and does not do with data.

1. Summary

  • We do not collect, transmit, or sell any data.
  • The App never connects to the network and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDK.
  • The App requests no permissions. It does not use your location. There is no account and no login.
  • The only thing stored is the aircraft profiles you create, and they stay on your device.

2. Data stored on your device

Only the aircraft profiles you choose to save: their name, station names, arms, weights and the CG limits you enter. They are written to this device's app storage, are never transmitted, and are deleted when you delete the profile or the App. The App writes no other files, no keychain entries, and no advertising identifier.

3. Data sent to us or to anyone else

None. The App contains no networking code, so there is no channel through which it could send anything, even by accident.

4. Purchases

Whizwheel is a paid app with no in-app purchases and no subscription. Your purchase is processed entirely by Apple. The App itself contains no purchase, payment, or analytics SDK. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles payment data.

5. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13/16. There are no ads, no data collection, and no third-party content.

6. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / APPI)

Because we neither collect nor store personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, port, or delete. Anything the App keeps is on your device and under your control: deleting the App removes it.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
